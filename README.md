## Project Overview

This project explores health patterns and data quality
in two US public health datasets.

The analysis focuses on two research questions:

1. Is sleep duration associated with systolic blood
   pressure among US adults?
2. Are waterborne disease outbreaks during summer
   associated with higher reported illness counts
   than outbreaks in other seasons?

## Datasets

### 1. NHANES

The National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey
provides information on health and lifestyle factors.

We will examine sleep duration, blood pressure,
age, gender, and BMI.

### 2. CDC Waterborne Outbreaks

CDC outbreak surveillance data provide information
on reported disease outbreaks in the United States.

We will explore seasonal and regional differences
in waterborne disease outbreaks.

## Methods

- Data cleaning and missing data analysis
- Descriptive statistics
- Data visualization with ggplot2
- Regression analysis
- Discussion of uncertainty and limitations
